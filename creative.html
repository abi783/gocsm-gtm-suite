Production

Shot list & rulesRecord once, in this order — everything shares setup.

One-day shoot order

Same camera, same light, same shirt — batch by setup, not by asset:

① Webinar cold open (3 takes, pick the loosest) → ② Ad 1 & Ad 2 talking-head segments → ③ Ad 3 + YouTube screen-captures (no face needed — record after lunch when energy dips) → ④ YouTube face-cam segments → ⑤ One spare take of everything saying "this week" instead of dates, for evergreen reuse.

The creative rules

• First 2 seconds = the claim, never a logo.
• Real dashboard, real data, real latency — polish reads as fake in this market.
• Captions burned in (85% watch muted).
• One CTA per asset. The masterclass, always — never the trial, never pricing.
• Stats discipline from Step 1 applies to every script: ranges, attributed, no "$640K" demo numbers.
