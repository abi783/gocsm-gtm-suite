Risk-Reversal Design · replaces the free trial

The Double
Guarantee.

With the trial gone from the live offer, the guarantee carries 100% of the risk-reversal load. One unconditional layer for trust, one conditional outcome layer for confidence — labor gated past the impulse window, refunds turned into save offers.

1 · B2B wants specificity

Agency owners don't fear "not liking the software" — they fear paying $970 and never setting it up. The promise must name the outcome (Hormozi: the more business the buyer, the more specific the guarantee).

2 · Cost is asymmetric

The software costs ~nothing to refund. The bonus stack (done-for-you setup, 1:1 session) is real labor — protect the labor, never weaken the headline.

3 · Boldness wins the math

Even when a stronger guarantee raises refunds, net sales rise more. Strong @10% conversion − 12% refunds beats weak @7% − 5% refunds by ~26% kept revenue.

The guarantee stackUnconditional for trust + conditional for confidence. Named, branded, gated.

Layer 1 · The trust layer — replaces the trial

The 30-Day "Delete-It-All" Guarantee

"Try GoCSM for 30 days. If it's not for you — for any reason, or no reason — email us and we refund 100%. No call, no exit interview, no hoops. And you keep the Churn Playbook, the templates, and everything you learned. We'll even help you export your data."
  • Window: 30 days from purchase — not from activation. Clean, unambiguous
  • Scope: full $970 refund (or all 3-pay installments paid to date)
  • Conditions: none. One email = refund within 5 business days
  • They keep: digital bonuses — playbook PDF, templates, recording
  • Not kept: the labor bonuses (see the gating rule)
  • Why unconditional: software marginal cost ≈ 0 — "no questions asked" is exactly what makes skipping the trial feel safe
Layer 2 · The confidence layer — the showstopper

The 60-Day "Red Flag" Guarantee

"Complete your onboarding call and connect your sub-accounts. If GoCSM hasn't flagged at least 3 genuinely at-risk client accounts you didn't already know about within 60 days, you choose: we work with you free until it does — or we refund the year AND you keep every bonus, including the done-for-you setup we already built."
  • Conditions = the activation plan: ① onboarding call within 14 days ② OAuth connected, ≥10 sub-accounts tracked
  • The promise: ≥3 at-risk accounts surfaced (health <40 or 2+ churn signals) the agency wasn't already managing
  • Remedy — their choice: free service until delivered, OR full refund + keep everything
  • Why it works: the Hormozi formula — if not X result in Y time, we do Z — and any 20+ account portfolio has at-risk accounts. That's the product's entire premise
  • The conditions force the behavior that prevents your #1 refund risk: churn-by-never-logging-in
  • Honest footnote: tiny/very-healthy portfolios → converts to "verified-healthy + top 3 expansion candidates, or refund." Never fail on a technicality

The combined webinar line

"You're covered twice. 30 days, any reason, full refund, keep the bonuses. And if you do the onboarding and we don't find at-risk revenue hiding in your accounts within 60 days — we work free or pay you back. The only way you lose money today is if you do nothing at all… which, ironically, is exactly how you're losing money right now."
Placement: Layer 1 in one breath → pause → Layer 2 as the escalation — immediately before the price reveal. The buyer hears $970 while feeling zero risk.

Quiet mechanics — never in the pitch

Anti-abuse & cost protection

⏱ Labor gating — the key move

Protection through choreography, invisible to the buyer

  • Two kinds of bonuses. Digital (playbook PDF, templates, recording) cost nothing — refunders keep them, which is what makes the guarantee feel generous. Labor (done-for-you setup, 1:1 build) costs real team hours
  • The trap: setup delivered week 1 → refund week 2 = you eat the refund AND the labor. Exactly Hormozi's high-fulfillment-cost warning
  • The fix is a schedule, not fine print: deliver labor on Days 7–21. Impulse refunds land in week 1 — flaky buyers self-select out before any labor is spent. The serious ones, who wait a week and show up, are who you invest in
On the webinar you say only: "we'll book your setup session right after purchase." Never explain the delay — naming it as refund protection would poison the generosity of the whole guarantee.
  • Day 0: purchase → digital bonuses delivered instantly, setup session booked
  • Days 1–7: impulse-refund window passes — no labor at risk
  • Days 7–21: done-for-you setup + 1:1 build delivered, inside the relationship
  • The deliberate exception: complete onboarding, receive the setup, then invoke Layer 2 → they keep the work. <2% ever will — and "keep the work even if we refund you" is what makes the Red Flag promise land as bold, not gimmick

💳 3-pay rules

Refund = installments paid to date, remaining installments canceled, access ends at refund. Windows run from first payment. No "refund payment 1, ride until payment 2 fails."

🧾 Chargeback prevention

Clear descriptor ("GOCSM ANNUAL"), refund email printed on the receipt, 5-day SLA. A fast, friendly refund is 10× cheaper than a dispute. One redemption per agency/payment method.

⚖️ FTC honesty rule

Every condition stated before purchase. Refund to original payment method. Terms on the checkout page itself — not buried in a T&C link. Digital bonuses never clawed back: it costs nothing and it's the line that makes the guarantee feel generous instead of legal.

Presentation choreographyWebinar → Q&A → checkout → close emails.

Q&A objection frames

"What if I don't have time to set it up?"
That's why setup is done for you — the Red Flag guarantee only asks you to show up to one call.
"What if my accounts are all healthy?"
Then you'll know that for certain for the first time — and if we can't show you 3 at-risk OR your top expansion plays, you get your money back.
"Why no free trial?"
A trial without onboarding is how people fail with software. You get the paid version of a trial: 30 days fully refundable, with our team actually setting you up. Less risk than a trial, not more.

Surface placement

  • Webinar: after the stack, before the price — risk reversal lands hardest right before the number
  • Checkout page: badge + one-liner per layer + "refunds in 5 business days" (the SLA is itself a trust signal)
  • Close email #3: the guarantee deep-dive — full terms, plainly written. Transparency converts the skeptics urgency can't

🛡️ The Double Guarantee

1 · The 30-Day Delete-It-All Guarantee. Any reason or none. One email, full refund in 5 business days, keep the Churn Playbook and templates.

2 · The 60-Day Red Flag Guarantee. Show up to your onboarding call and connect your accounts. If GoCSM doesn't surface at least 3 at-risk client accounts you didn't know about, you pick: we work free until it does — or full refund and you keep everything, including your done-for-you setup.

The only losing move is doing nothing.

One-line summary: an unconditional 30-day "keep-the-bonuses" refund builds the trust a trial used to provide · a conditional 60-day "3 at-risk accounts or we pay you" promise sells the outcome · labor bonuses sit past the impulse window · one save offer at the refund gate quietly recovers a third of would-be refunds.
