The Gainsight-era gap is the lag between "score drops" and "customer hears from you." The fix: the workflow responds in minutes, the CSM responds in hours, the founder responds in days — and severity decides which.

The severity modelEvery fire is triaged into one of four lanes before anyone acts.

LaneDefinitionResponse SLAWho acts
🟢 Green · ExpansionPositive signal (engagement up, health ≥80)This weekWorkflow + CSM (upsell motion)
🔵 Blue · NudgeSingle early signal on a healthy accountAutomated onlyWorkflow; CSM sees digest
🟡 Yellow · InterveneMid signal, or 2 signals on one account48 hoursWorkflow + CSM task
🔴 Red · RescueHealth <40, renewal <60d at risk, payment failed ×2, or 3+ signalsSame dayCSM call + founder visibility
The compounding rule from the masterclass applies here too: one signal = watch (blue), two = act this week (yellow), three+ = that account is canceling (red). Use accountPriority on every trigger instance to fast-lane your top-MRR accounts.

Rescue play 1 · involuntary churn — the most fixable kind

Payment FailedPolls every 3h. 20–40% of all churn is involuntary; dunning with direct card-links converts 2.3× better.

The Dunning Rescue

saas_payment_failed · payload: failureReason, paymentType, amount, ownerEmail
🔴 Red if ×2 · 🟡 first failure
⚙️ Configure
  • Instance 1: all plans, subscription payments
  • Instance 2: accountPriority = true → separate fast-lane workflow
  • revisitInterval 1d — repeat failures must re-fire
🤖 Automated (minutes)
  • T+0: friendly email — assume innocence, direct card-update link (2.3× conversion)
  • T+48h: SMS nudge (4 retries / 3–4 emails / 1 SMS over 14 days is the benchmark sequence)
  • Tag account payment-risk; never suspend silently
👤 CSM (same day if priority)
  • Priority or >$300/mo account: personal call within 24h — "card hiccup, not a collections call"
  • 2nd failure: check health score — low health + failed pay = a cancellation in progress, open a save play
  • Never let the contractor say "I figured you didn't notice I was gone"
Escalate: 3rd failure or downgrade request → founder outreach. Metric: recovery rate (target 60%+), time-to-recovery, % suspended-without-contact = 0.

Rescue play 2 · the composite signal

Health Score ChangedFires daily post-scoring. The payload carries per-dimension sub-scores — that's your diagnosis, not just your alarm.

The Diagnostic Save

health_score_changed · payload: pasScore, revenueScore, loginScore, npsScore, scoreHistory, lifecycleStage
🔴 score_below 40 · 🟡 score_down_by 10
⚙️ Configure (3 instances)
  • score_down_by 10, window 7d → yellow lane
  • score_below 40, window 1d, revisit 30d → red lane
  • score_above 80 → green lane (expansion — see stacks)
🤖 Automated
  • Yellow: value-recap email — "here's what your system did this month" (leads, bookings)
  • Red: book-a-call link + CSM task with sub-scores in the task body
  • Tag with the falling dimension (adoption/revenue/sentiment/login)
👤 CSM — diagnose by dimension
  • pasScore fell → adoption play: re-onboard the abandoned feature
  • revenueScore fell → upstream problem: their ad spend / lead flow dried up — fix the input, not the tool
  • npsScore fell → call, don't email. A detractor who's talking is still saveable
  • loginScore alone → lowest weight, watch only
Escalate: red + renewal <60d → drop everything (the rescue stack). Metric: % of red accounts contacted same-day; 30-day score recovery rate.

Rescue play 3 · the deadline every other signal feeds

Upcoming Plan RenewalhealthStatus filter makes this three different plays. T-90 starts achieve 15–20% higher renewal rates; late saves cost ~12% in discounts.

The Renewal Ladder

upcoming_plan_renewal · payload: renewalDate, daysUntilRenewal, healthBand, paymentFrequency
Lane depends on healthBand
⚙️ Configure (3 instances)
  • T-30 · all bands → pipeline entry + health check digest
  • T-30 · at_risk + watch → save play, annual plans first
  • T-7 · thriving + healthy → anniversary / expansion touch
🤖 Automated
  • T-30 healthy: "your year in numbers" ROI recap email — renewal framed as momentum, not invoice
  • T-30 at-risk: no renewal mention yet — value-first re-engagement + booking link
  • T-7: confirm card on file is current (pre-dunning prevents 35–40% of expiry failures)
👤 CSM — the green/yellow/red ladder
  • Green: lightweight — thank-you + expansion question
  • Yellow: consultative QBR — usage review in their language (leads/bookings/revenue vs fee)
  • Red: save play — diagnose with sub-scores, fix the named issue, re-onboard. A discount is the last lever, not the first
Escalate: red at T-30 → founder/exec sponsor call (the documented red-account playbook). Metric: on-time renewal rate (benchmark lift 72→94%), save rate on at_risk renewals, discount % conceded.

Adoption play 1 · relationship risk, not just usage risk

Inactivity AlertPer-user per-location. A key user gone dark is single-point-of-failure risk — multi-thread before they leave.

The Re-Engagement Ladder

inactivity_alert · payload: userRole, isKeyUser, daysSinceLastLogin, userEmail
🔵 7d · 🟡 key user 14d · 🔴 owner 30d
⚙️ Configure (3 instances)
  • 7d · all users → automated nudge only, revisit 30d
  • 14d · isKeyUser = true → CSM task
  • 30d · role = admin → red lane, owner is checked out
🤖 Automated
  • 7d: "while you were away" email — what the system did for them (appointments booked, leads captured)
  • 14d: feature-specific win + 1-click login link
  • Login activity is only 10% of health — never panic on this alone
👤 CSM
  • Key user 14d: personal video message — 60 seconds, their account on screen
  • Owner 30d: call. The quiet-phase breakup starts here — slower replies, fewer approvals
  • Key user left the company? → re-onboard the replacement within 7 days (highest-risk moment in the account's life)
Metric: reactivation rate within 14 days of fire; % of accounts with ≥2 active users (multi-threading coverage).

Adoption play 2 · the velocity drop — the Day 45–75 predictor

Feature Engagement ChangedWoW/MoM volume per feature. Decreased 30% = the most reliable early churn predictor; increased = expansion fuel.

The Velocity Check

feature_engagement_changed · payload: featureName, wow/mom old/new values, direction
🟡 −30% WoW · 🟢 +50% MoM
⚙️ Configure (2 instances)
  • decreased · 30% · WoW → yellow lane
  • increased · 50% · MoM → green lane (expansion signal)
  • Scope to revenue features: Workflows, SMS, Email, Payments
🤖 Automated
  • Decrease: feature-specific tip + template ("3 reactivation campaigns that book appointments")
  • Increase: congratulate with the numbers — make the win visible to the owner
👤 CSM — find the upstream cause
  • Volume drops are usually upstream: ad spend cut, staff change, seasonal dip — ask before prescribing
  • The gym story: 400→220→90 SMS wasn't a GHL problem, it was a lead-flow problem. Solve their business problem and you're unfireable
  • Increase + healthy: feed the expansion stack
Metric: % of −30% fires that recover volume within 30 days; expansion conversations opened from +50% fires.

Adoption play 3 · depth, not volume

Feature Time-Spent ChangedMinutes-in-feature, 12 features. Falling time-spent with stable volume = they're disengaging from the tool itself.

The Depth Gauge

feature_time_spent_changed · payload: featureName, wow/mom mins, direction
🔵 −50% MoM · 🟢 +75% on new feature
⚙️ Configure
  • decreased · 50% · MoM — looser than engagement (time data is noisier)
  • increased · 75% on Dashboards/Reputation/Memberships → someone is exploring — strike while warm
🤖 Automated
  • Decrease: pair with engagement data before acting — time alone is blue-lane
  • Increase on unowned feature: send the setup guide for exactly that feature within the hour
👤 CSM
  • Exploring a premium-adjacent feature (Memberships, Reputation)? → 15-min "want me to set this up with you?" offer — the easiest expansion call you'll ever book
  • Time falling across all features → treat as Signal 3 (ghost account), check stale assets
Metric: setup-call bookings from increase-fires; % of all-feature declines escalated to ghost-account review.

Adoption play 4 · built but never switched on

Asset Activation PendingA drafted workflow, a bought phone number, an unverified email domain — friction sitting between the client and first value.

The Activation Sweep

asset_activation_pending · payload: featureName, assetName, daysSinceCreated
🔵 3d · 🟡 14d on core assets
⚙️ Configure (2 instances)
  • More than 3 days · Workflows + Phone Numbers + Email Domains → nudge
  • More than 14 days · all features → CSM task
🤖 Automated
  • 3d: "your {assetName} is one click from live" — deep link + 2-min how-to video for that asset type
  • Unverified email domain / unconfigured phone = their campaigns literally can't send — flag as blocking
👤 CSM
  • 14d: offer to finish it for them on a 15-min screen-share — done-for-you beats one more tutorial
  • Multiple pending assets on one account = an onboarding that stalled, not a lazy client — restart the onboarding track
Metric: activation rate within 7 days of fire; median days-to-activation by asset type.

Adoption play 5 · the onboarding gate (time)

Low Time-Spent Since SignupAge-gated: fires when a new account hasn't invested X minutes by Day Y. This is your time-to-first-value alarm.

The TTFV Gate

gocsm_feature_time_spent_less_than_x_since_signup · payload: lowEngagementFeatures, daysSinceSignup
🟡 Day 7 · 🔴 Day 14 unresolved
⚙️ Configure (2 instances)
  • Day 7 · <30 min on Workflows + Calendars + Conversations
  • Day 14 · <30 min → red-lane re-onboard, revisit 30d
🤖 Automated
  • Day 7: restart the onboarding email track for the listed features — short, single-CTA steps
  • Book-a-setup-call link in every touch
👤 CSM
  • Day 14: call, don't email. Completed onboarding churns ~8% at 90 days vs ~25% incomplete — this call is the highest-ROI hour in the book
  • Re-anchor to their first-value moment: first booked appointment, first lead — not "finish the setup"
Metric: % of new accounts hitting first value by Day 30 (target ≥60%); Day-14 fire rate trending down month over month.

Adoption play 6 · the onboarding gate (breadth)

Assets Empty Since SignupFires when whole features were never explored by Day Y. Multi-feature users retain ~70% better — this is the breadth alarm.

The Breadth Builder

gocsm_feature_assets_empty_since_signup · payload: emptyFeatures, emptyCount, daysSinceSignup
🟡 Day 7 ≥3 empty · 🔴 Day 30 ≥5 empty
⚙️ Configure (2 instances)
  • Day 7 · all 8 features → map the gap
  • Day 30 → escalation lane for never-launched accounts
🤖 Automated
  • One feature at a time — never "here are 5 things you haven't done." Pick the highest-value empty feature for their vertical (gym → calendars; contractor → phone; realtor → reputation)
  • Template-first: "here's a pre-built workflow, click import"
👤 CSM
  • Day 30 ≥5 empty: this client bought software they never unboxed — full re-onboarding session, treat as a brand-new account
  • Pair with the agency's delivery checklist: empty features sometimes mean the agency never built what was sold (the expectations time-bomb)
Metric: features-adopted-by-Day-30 distribution; % of accounts at 3+ features (the retention cliff edge).

Where the system beats single alerts

Composite stacksSingle triggers are signals. Combinations are verdicts — wire these as tag-based meta-plays in GHL.

🚨 The Rescue Stack

health <40 + renewal <60d (+ any payment failure)

  • Drop everything — this is a cancellation in progress
  • CSM call same day; founder visibility same week
  • Fix the named issue first; discount last

📈 The Expansion Stack

score_above 80 + engagement/time increased + renewal upcoming

  • The 5 churn signals in reverse = upsell-ready
  • Offer the next tier / more locations at the T-30 touch
  • Also: case-study & referral ask — promoters convert at 60–70%

👻 The Ghost Stack

inactivity 30d + time-spent down all features + no new assets

  • The account is on life support — old automations mask it
  • Relationship play, not feature play: "what changed in your business?"
  • 45–90 day fuse — longest warning you'll ever get. Use it

🐣 The Launch Stack

low time-spent Day 7 + assets empty Day 7 + asset pending

  • Onboarding has stalled in week one — intervene before habit windows close
  • Goal: first value ≤14 days (the master retention metric)
  • One feature, one win, celebrated loudly

The CSM weekly cadenceTriggers feed the queue; this is how a human works it.

Daily (30 min)

  • Red lane first — same-day SLA: rescue stacks, payment ×2, score <40
  • Scan yellow queue, claim tasks, book calls
  • Log every outcome — it feeds tomorrow's score

Weekly (90 min)

  • Portfolio review: sort by health band, check band migrations
  • Expansion stack review — who moved to green this week?
  • Fire-rate review: which trigger fired most? That's this month's systemic fix

Effort follows lead time

Where CSM hours go, by lane — rescue is expensive, prevention is cheap. The system's job is shifting the mix left.
