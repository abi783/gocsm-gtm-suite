Why annual-first

  • Annual churns at ⅓ the monthly rate (~2.4% vs ~7%/mo)
  • Stays ~40 months vs ~14 · 2.3× more likely to upgrade
  • "12 for the price of 10" — instantly recognized from GHL
  • 16.7% discount sits in the optimal 15–20% band
  • All cash lands on Day 0

The webinar offerLead with the paid annual. No trial visible live — it absorbs the buyers.

The stack — value first, price last

  • GoCSM Annual — 12 months, up to 100 subs
  • 🎁 Fast-action: done-for-you setup & workflow build
  • 🎁 1:1 onboarding session (the old trial CTA, now a bonus)
  • 🎁 Template library — login-drop, NPS, failed-payment, renewal triggers
  • 🎁 First 10 buyers: dedicated CSM session (seeds L3)
  • Reveal: $970/yr or 3 × $388
  • Risk reversal: 30-day keep-the-bonuses guarantee

Urgency — must be real (FTC)

  • Fast-action bonus for buying live / first 24h
  • Cart closes 48–72h after the webinar — hard, enforced
  • 3–4 emails on close day (final-day ≈ 2× the money)
  • No fake timers, no resetting countdowns
Close: "If GoCSM saves even ONE client from churning, it's paid for itself for the year."

CashflowPer 100 live attendees, net of ~8–10% refunds, 90-day window.

Recommendations

1 · Build all three this sprint

~70% shared slides — only hook, framing, demo & reg page change.

2 · Instrument the full funnel first

UTM every link; track live buyers & paid-on-deadline. Send replay within 2–4h.

3 · Weight paid buyers & L3 attach

If fear fills the room but authority fills the bank — run authority live.

4 · No demo numbers as real results

Use third-party benchmarks + verifiable facts (#7 most-installed; 38,100+ sub-accounts).

Thresholds: no variant clears ~30% reg → the pain is wrong. Reg strong, attendee→buyer weak → the demo. Paid conversion weak everywhere → the offer.
