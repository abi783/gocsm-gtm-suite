A · The Agency Builder

  • Versioned journey mechanism — edit safely, assign per sub-account
  • Guided 4-step wizard, not a blank editor
  • Paste-checklist AI import — agency pastes their existing onboarding doc, AI drafts the journey, human approves
  • White-label theming — the client never sees "GoCSM"

B · The Doer Widget

  • Lives inside the sub-account — non-blocking, progress-visible
  • One current-step card: do this next, here's why, here's how
  • The signature mechanic — "it just knew": live detectors mark steps done the moment the client actually does them. No checkbox theater

C · The Stall Dashboard

  • Verdict: AI-attributed, trust-calibrated portfolio readout
  • Queue: stalled accounts, triaged — the centerpiece
  • Evidence: which step kills momentum across all clients
  • Triggers: stalled-step → HighLevel custom trigger → workflow + CSM task

Detectors & delivery wavesSteps are only as good as their detection. Three tiers of detectability, shipped in three research-backed waves.

The three detectability tiers

  • Tier 1 · Auto-detected: ClickHouse syncs + live read-on-focus — step completes itself (pipeline created, calendar live, number purchased)
  • Tier 2 · Assisted: system sees a strong proxy, asks for one-tap confirmation
  • Tier 3 · Manual: human attests (e.g. "kickoff call held") — kept rare by design
Detection freshness: nightly scan + read-on-focus live verification — the widget re-checks the moment the client opens it, so "it just knew" stays true.

The prioritized step catalog

  • Wave 1 · Communication rails — the universal blockers: phone number, email domain, calendar connected. Nothing works until these do
  • Wave 2 · Lead capture & conversion — pipeline, forms/funnels, first workflow, first contact import
  • Wave 3 · Integrations & human anchor — payments, review/reputation, the kickoff call
Wave 1 maps 1:1 to the webinar's "time-to-first-ring" teaching — the product ships the same priorities the masterclass preaches.

The 10th play — extends the Trigger Playbook

The Onboarding Stall playThe new custom trigger the activation system adds — Signal 0, automated.

⚙️ Configure

  • Step stalled >5 days in Wave 1 → yellow lane
  • Journey <50% at Day 21 → red lane
  • Journey complete → 🟢 celebration + review-ask workflow

🤖 Automated

  • Stalled step → white-label nudge with the exact next action + how-to
  • Wave-1 blocker stalled → escalate immediately: their campaigns literally can't send
  • Day-21 red → book-a-setup-call link, CSM task with the stalled step named

👤 CSM

  • Red lane: 15-min "we'll finish it for you" screen-share — done-for-you beats one more tutorial
  • Evidence view weekly: the step that stalls most clients is the journey design problem to fix
  • Completion = a logged win — celebrate it and feed the case-study bank

The daily AI-CSM routineFive prompts that replace an hour of dashboard archaeology — and the webinar's most demo-able moment.

› Which of my accounts went at-risk this week, and why — by health dimension?
› Any failed payments or renewals inside 30 days on accounts below health 60?
› Who are my top 5 upsell candidates and what signal makes each one ready?
› Which onboarding step is stalling the most new clients right now?
› Draft the check-in message for FitZone and send it.

Why this matters commercially

  • Webinar demo: after the dashboard tour, one live "ask Claude about my portfolio" moment — no competitor in the GHL space can show this
  • Differentiator: GoCSM isn't a report, it's a data spine any AI can operate
  • L3 delivery: the retainer team runs on the same routine — MCP is what makes $500/mo for ~4 hrs profitable
  • Content engine: the routine is itself YouTube material (the AI-CSM video project)

Sherlock — the conversational assistPhase 4 of the activation plan: the MCP brain, embedded in the product.

Same tools, no setup. Sherlock is the in-dashboard conversational layer over the stall analytics — "why is Smile Dental stuck?" answered with the evidence trail. MCP serves the power users today; Sherlock brings the identical capability to every agency owner who'll never configure an MCP client.
